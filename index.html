Plato

The Greek dialogues in the order of Socrates’ life, from the young man who met Parmenides to the day of his death. The nine tetralogies remain as a second view.

Greek with a facing English translation and commentary. The first is Alcibiades I, read in the full Sefarium reader.

About Sefarium

Sefarium is a free reading of Greek dialogues: the text, a translation, and notes on one page. The library begins with Plato.
