Πλάτων

The dialogues, in Greek

The thirty-six works of Plato in the Perseus collection, in John Burnet’s Oxford text, arranged in the nine Thrasyllan tetralogies. Each page is the Greek only, with Stephanus pages and sections, and with speaker names where the edition marks them.

Works tagged dubium are those the tradition has usually doubted: Alcibiades II, Hipparchus, the Lovers, Theages, Cleitophon, Minos, and Epinomis. The Letters are included; they are not all regarded as genuine.
